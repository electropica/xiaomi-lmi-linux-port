# Linux 7 automatic suspend and boot menu, 4 November 2026

An unattended unplugged trial began at approximately 55% displayed capacity. With the Mobian user's idle action set to suspend after 900 seconds on both battery and AC, the phone entered `s2idle`; the suspend-success counter advanced from 0 to 1 and the sleep hook recorded approximately 11 minutes between the pre- and post-suspend boundaries. The display backlight was off at both boundaries. The post-suspend observation already reported charging after reconnection, and the gauge showed 54% before suspend and 53% after. This is not an integrated current measurement, a matched endurance comparison, or evidence of hardware deep sleep or a 20% energy saving. Cause of the previous night's failure to suspend remains unknown. On an awake unplug/replug check, the charger and TCPM online states and UPower battery state changed consistently with the cable; this does not establish the earlier night's state.

Setting AC idle action to suspend is a reversible workaround which also permits idle suspension while connected to a real charger. The temporary system-sleep collector was removed after matching its hash and preserving its log with matching device and local hashes.

A separate boot-menu recipe was built with Linux 7 as default and the Linux 4.19 menu entry hidden and disabled; Android/Lineage, fastboot and recovery entries remain in the source. The output SHA-256 is `11868e57e56f9a51936c14a6456bcc2d430e5382d3febaf5785a450ae362ab32`. Host verification checked the Android image header, component hashes, original bundle, source manifest and menu settings. The existing Linux 7 kernel and DTB are unchanged. The Linux 4.19 payload remains in the bundle; the previous menu image remains available as rescue. The new menu has not been installed or tested on the device.

The Mobian user data and settings are shared between kernels. An inventory and a separately verified backup are prerequisites for planning a separate installation; no repartitioning or replacement has been performed for this purpose.
