# Cleanup and reconstruction continuity — October 23, 2026

The permanent dualboot menu and compatible rescue were preserved, together
with the latest installable Mobian image. The menu has four operator-tested
entries and defaults to Mobian after fifteen seconds. Cleanup did not change
the installed phone or start another hardware test.

## Removed material

- Superseded rootfs builds, menu experiments, old package outputs and unused
  local model downloads.
- Obsolete source clones and toolchain caches, after identifying local changes
  and preserving the required external inputs.
- The old mainline kernel clone, distinct from the retained Lineage 4.19 RPMh
  kernel. All its refs were remote-reachable, its stash was empty and its two
  tracked modifications plus untracked instructions matched the external archive.
- Redundant Git bundles. Published ancestry, rewritten patch identities and
  exact public upstream revisions were checked; unique small patches and
  metadata were retained outside Git.
- A complete external source copy. Its final audit classified 94,066 entries:
  93,946 public regular-file blobs, 99 public symlink targets, two saved source
  modifications and 19 generated cache files. Link targets were verified through
  Linux and links themselves were removed before recursive Windows deletion.

Private receipts contain the exact paths, hashes and decisions. They and raw
observations remain outside the public repository.

## Reconstruction repairs

The dualboot build and finish wrappers no longer depend on a removed Downloads
copy. They use the retained original source boot, allow DUALBOOT_SOURCE_BOOT
to override its location and verify its pinned SHA256 before proceeding. The
compatible rescue is not an interchangeable source input. Seven preparation
tests and shell syntax checks passed without an image or kernel build.

The historical archi-validation-02 lock no longer claims its old external paths
are present. Its RAM boot was verified in the external archive; the exact old
sparse and rootfs were not found. Their disappearance date and cause are not
established. Embedded payloads must not be assumed extractible from a different
RAM boot. Manifest-only verification passes; full verification rejects
unavailable artifacts. This historical limitation does not replace or invalidate
the current build reference.

## Remaining working material

Active native kernel/pmbootstrap, menu, userspace and camera environments remain
at their existing paths. Downloadable dependencies still used by these recipes
are working tools, rather than redundant external backups. An unrelated local
assistant project was left in place. The remaining small historical preservation
folder was not discarded without establishing its full content provenance.

WSL filesystem usage was measured separately from its host VHDX. The filesystem
uses approximately 37 GiB; the VHDX remains approximately 119 GiB before
compaction. Host compaction has not been performed: it requires Administrator
PowerShell after WSL jobs have finished. No space saving on the host is claimed
until the VHDX has actually been compacted and remeasured.
